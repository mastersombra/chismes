❌ **Illegality:**

❌ **Misinformation:**
- Seriously false news
- Public health misinformation

### 5.2. Consequences

Every chisme rejected by a moderator is a strike. Strikes add points according to their severity, and points expire after 90 days:
- Low: 1 point
- Medium: 2 points
- High: 4 points
- Critical: immediate suspension

Based on your active points:
- Fewer than 3: warning
- From 3: 24 hours without posting or reporting
- From 6: 7 days without posting or reporting
- From 10: indefinite suspension (only lifted by an accepted appeal)

Automatic rejections do not add points. If you delete your account and sign up again with the same email, your previous strikes still count. We may report illegal content to the authorities.

You can see your status in Profile → Account status.

### 5.3. Appeals

If you believe your content was removed in error:
- Appeal from Profile → Account status, within 14 days of the rejection
- One appeal per rejected chisme
- If accepted: the strike is voided and the chisme text is published again (photos are not restored)
